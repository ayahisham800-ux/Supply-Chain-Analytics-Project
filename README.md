# 📦 Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics (DEPI Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing end-to-end *Supply Chain and Logistics operations* to optimize shipping costs, evaluate supplier quality, and enhance inventory management. By leveraging data analytics and business intelligence tools, this project aims to identify operational bottlenecks and support data-driven decision-making.

> *Note:* This repository currently holds the initial project structure and documentation. Final dashboards, scripts, and analysis files will be updated progressively.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview
The analysis is built on the *Supply Chain Analysis Dataset* (supply_chain_data.csv), consisting of *100 records* and *24 attributes* across three main areas:
- *Sales & Products:* Product type, SKU, Price, Number of products sold, Revenue generated, Customer demographics.
- *Manufacturing & Inventory:* Stock levels, Lead times, Order quantities, Manufacturing costs, Inspection results, Defect rates.
- *Logistics & Shipping:* Shipping carriers, Shipping times, Shipping costs, Transportation modes (Air, Road, Rail, Sea), Routes, Location.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- *Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:* Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Python (Pandas)
- *Data Querying:* SQL
- *Data Visualization & BI:* Microsoft Power BI (DAX & Data Modeling)

---

## 🎯 Key Analysis Pillars & KPIs
1. *Sales & Revenue Performance:* Tracking total revenue, units sold, and profit margins across product categories (Skincare, Haircare, Cosmetics).
2. *Logistics & Carrier Efficiency:* Comparing shipping times and transportation costs across carriers (Carrier A, B, C) and modes (Road, Air, Rail, Sea).
3. *Supplier & Quality Control:* Monitoring manufacturing costs, production lead times, and defect rates by supplier.
